# 🇮🇳 Indian Polity and Governance — Civil Services Exam Preparation Guide

> A comprehensive, chapter-wise repository of study notes, revision guides, and solved previous years' question papers tailored for Central and State Civil Services Examinations (UPSC, UP-PCS, BPSC, MPPSC, RPSC, and more).

---

## 📖 About the Resource
This material is the English adaptation of the renowned **Purvavalokun (पूर्वावलोकन) - Indian Polity and Governance** series. It bridges the gap for aspirants by consolidating over **2 decades of examination trends**, packed with exhaustive chapter-wise revision notes and authenticated multiple-choice questions (MCQs) with detailed analytical explanations.

---
